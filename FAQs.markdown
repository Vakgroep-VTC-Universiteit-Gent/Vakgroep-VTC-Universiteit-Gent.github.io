### Ghent University-wide policy on GenAI?

The policy on generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) in Ghent University educational practices is explained in detail in a [dedicated *onderwijstip*](https://onderwijstips.ugent.be/nl/tips/chatgpt-een-generatief-ai-systeem-met-impact-op-he/). As of the academic year 2025-2026, there is also a dedicated landing page (www.ugent.be/genai) where the core aspects of the university's policy on GenAI are described (both for educational and research purposes).

### Faculty of Arts and Philosophy policy on GenAI?

All information and documentation related to the faculty policy on generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) is gathered in a [dedicated submodule](https://ufora.ugent.be/d2l/le/content/72825/Home) of the [faculty's info site](https://ufora.ugent.be/d2l/home/72825) on Ufora. Among other documents, the submodule contains the faculty guidelines on responsible use of GenAI and the transparency declaration that needs to be handed in with every writing task.
